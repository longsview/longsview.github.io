Skymoji Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Skymoji shows the weather as animated emoji scenes on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. This page explains what data the app uses and who else sees it.

Your location

If you allow it, Skymoji uses your location while the app is open to show the weather where you are and to build the daily quiz. Your coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit and MapKit services to get the forecast and your city's name. The quiz is built on your device. We don't store or collect your location.

Your cities and settings

The cities you add are saved on your device and synced through your own iCloud account. Your settings, quiz streaks, custom emoji and Genmoji stay on your devices; your iPhone sends them to your paired Apple Watch directly. We can't see any of them.

Ads and tracking

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as an advertising identifier, IP address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first. The app also shows Apple's tracking prompt; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared and ads are not personalized. You can change both choices later. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Skymoji+ removes all ads.

Analytics and crash reports

Skymoji uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which features are used and to fix crashes. They receive events such as "forecast opened" or "quiz finished", crash details, and device information like model and iOS version, tied to a random app identifier rather than to you. We never send names, cities or locations with these events.

Purchases

Skymoji+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment, and the app only checks on your device whether you have an active purchase. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for anything beyond running the app, fixing problems and showing ads as described above.

Children

Skymoji isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off location access, the tracking permission and notifications at any time in iOS Settings. To ask about or delete any data held by Google for this app, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
