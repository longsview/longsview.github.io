Skymoji Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email getskymoji@gmail.com and include your device and iOS version. We usually answer within two days.

Common questions

My Location shows the wrong place or keeps searching.
Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Skymoji is set to While Using the App. Then pull down on the weather to refresh.
My cities aren't on my other devices.
Cities sync through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and iCloud Drive is on. It can take a minute.
How do I restore Skymoji+ on a new device?
Open Settings in Skymoji and tap Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel my subscription?
In Skymoji, open Settings › Manage Subscription, or go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
The Apple Watch app doesn't show my emoji or scene pack.
Open Skymoji on your iPhone with your watch nearby. The phone sends your settings to the watch.
How do I use a Genmoji in my scenes?
With Skymoji+, go to Settings › Your own emoji, pick one, then search in the emoji keyboard and tap Create New Emoji. Genmoji needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
Can I turn off the morning quiz reminder?
Yes. Open the quiz and turn off Remind me each morning.
