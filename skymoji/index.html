Skymoji

The weather as animated emoji scenes for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Open Skymoji and the sky moves: rain falls as 💧, snow drifts as ❄️, and the moon and stars come out at night. Swipe up for the hourly and 10-day forecast, and swipe sideways through your cities.

  • Live emoji scenes that follow the time of day and the weather
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch widgets
  • Skymoji Daily, a five-question geography quiz about where you are
  • Share your sky as a video, GIF or photo
  • Skymoji+: no ads, unlimited cities, seasonal scene packs, and your own emoji or Genmoji
